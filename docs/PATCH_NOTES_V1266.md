# DungeonDex v1.26.6

## Ashen Anvil Reinforcements

The Ashen Anvil now supports an additional upgrade path for an equipped Offhand.

### Offhand Upgrades

- Equip an Offhand, then visit the Lowfire Market to reinforce it.
- Each tier grants `+1 Guard` and `+1 Wit`.
- Offhands use the established three-tier path: `50c`, `125c`, and `250c`.
- The maximum upgrade level remains `+3`.
- The upgrade appears in Town, Gear, and the Guild Journal only while an Offhand is equipped.

### Existing Upgrades, Preserved

- Weapon upgrades remain `+2 Power` per tier.
- Armor upgrades remain `+2 Guard` and `+8 HP` per tier.
- Existing copper costs and the `+3` cap are unchanged.

### Town Shortcuts

- Hold the Town button in the side rail to reveal quick links to the Lowfire Market, Lowfire Forge, and Elite Contracts.
- Hold Town again to close the shortcut menu.

### Stable Foundations

This update does not change combat formulas, monster or boss scaling, rewards, drop rates, prices, save compatibility, dungeon entry, Debt, Talent, progression requirements, or Trophy Echo availability.
