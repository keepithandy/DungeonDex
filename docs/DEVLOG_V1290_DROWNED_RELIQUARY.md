# DungeonDex v1.29.0 — Drowned Reliquary

The Hollow Stair opens onto black water, prayer-ribbons, and bells that should have stopped ringing long ago.

Drowned Reliquary is a full new chapter for DungeonDex: a deeper, flooded stretch of the descent with its own enemies, elite dangers, gear identity, discoveries, and a final toll to answer.

## A Reliquary That Feels Like a Place

From D31 through D40, the Reliquary now has a complete encounter roster. Its drowned wardens, masked watchers, bell-bound hunters, and elite variants use the same combat rules as the rest of the dungeon, but carry their own silhouettes, names, and atmosphere.

The deeper you go, the more the chapter reveals itself: flooded halls, submerged offerings, and old vows caught in the dark.

## Choices Between the Fights

The Reliquary introduces three chapter incidents during a run:

- Follow a bell-rope down into a sealed vault.
- Decide what to do with a coffer buried beneath the silt.
- Pay—or refuse—the price of the Blackwater Tithe.

Each choice fits into the existing expedition flow. You can risk health for a cache, take an unsecured salvage haul, recover your breath, spend Ember for a better return, or simply move on.

## The Seventh Toll

At D40, the chapter reaches its final threshold.

Before the last room, the Seventh-Toll Bell-Keeper demands an answer. Take a final Reliquary cache, seal the threshold for a smaller haul, or pass beneath the bell and face what waits beyond. Your choice changes the moment, not the core rules of the descent.

## Gear Claimed From the Water

Reliquary finds now carry a distinct chapter identity across the existing gear system. They still use familiar slots, rarities, comparisons, upgrades, selling, and crafting support—just with names and details worthy of a drowned holy vault.

## A Chapter the Guild Remembers

The Guild Journal, Archive, Town, Notice Board, Contracts, and post-run records recognize the Reliquary journey using the systems already in place. Your run is easier to read and remember without turning the chapter into a separate progression track.

Trophy Echo remains the active Revisit lane. The Reliquary adds no competing memory mode and does not replace the trophies you have already earned.

## What Stays Steady

Drowned Reliquary is content added to the DungeonDex you already know:

- Combat timing, damage, statuses, and turn flow are unchanged.
- Loot rules, rarity behavior, rewards, merchant upgrades, Talents, Debt, and Charters are unchanged.
- Dungeon entry, extraction, replay, and saves remain familiar.
- The chapter stays comfortable on narrow screens, with the same touch and accessibility care as the rest of the game.

The bells are ringing. The water is waiting.
