# DungeonDex v1.27

## The Contract Board

The Guild now posts clearer named hunts for dangerous elites. Choose a contract, descend with a purpose, recognize your mark in combat, and return with proof of the kill.

### Clearer Elite Contracts

- Compare three named hunts directly on the Lowfire Elite Board.
- Each card shows its target, location, objective, risk, Bonus Writ, and established payout.
- Glassfang, Ash-Crowned, and Cinderjaw now carry distinct briefings and hunt identity.
- Only one contract can be active at a time.

### Recognize Your Mark

- The exact active target receives a compact `CONTRACT TARGET` badge in combat.
- A restrained ember-gold edge and short encounter cue make the mark recognizable without covering combat.
- Reduced-motion settings remove the brief entrance emphasis.
- Ordinary elites, bosses, and nonmatching targets do not receive the contract cue.

### Fulfillment and Records

- Target defeated, ready to claim, claimed, failed, and expired states are now easier to distinguish.
- Completed hunts remain claimable after saving and reloading.
- The established reward can still be claimed only once.
- Guild Journal cards preserve the named target, location, outcome, and available Bonus Writ result as read-only history.

### Mobile and Release Reliability

- Contract cards remain readable in a one-column phone layout.
- Runtime, contrast, touch geometry, package extraction, and protected-system checks now cover the complete hunt path.
- Available contract cards now show their resolved Floor, Room, and Chapter instead of an unknown floor.

### Gameplay Preserved

This update does not change combat formulas, monster or boss scaling, reward values, drop rates, economy, Merchant Gear Upgrade values or cap, save schema, dungeon entry, Debt, Talent, progression, or Trophy Echo-only Revisit behavior.
